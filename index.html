Our Story

A Passion for Food, A Legacy of Taste

✦

Saveur was born from a simple belief — that great food has the power to unite people. Founded in 1998 by Chef Arjun Mehta, our restaurant has grown from a small 20-seat eatery into one of Delhi's most celebrated dining destinations.

Every dish on our menu tells a story — of travel, of family recipes passed down through generations, of local farmers who grow our ingredients with love.
